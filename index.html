But the monster they"ve come to catch doesn"t plot, doesn"t plan, doesn"t scheme. He just stands at every death scene, grinning like it"s the greatest show on earth. A physiological thriller where the enemy isn"t a mastermind — it"s a madman who finds beauty in death.
